## AWS Skills Development Bursary 2027 — closed

- **Official opportunity page:** <https://studytrust.org.za/amazon-web/>
- **Application destination linked from that page:** <https://studytrust.kycdd.co.za/client/-/insert/subscription_id/9ff211fb-b816-470a-a41f-300fea952f78/workflow_id/a117b2f8-4574-482f-a323-fac07ef53661>
- **Current status:** Closed for 2027. StudyTrust’s directory says “Now closed for 2027”; the individual page still shows the extended season ending 16 October 2026. The portal hides it because its deadline passed and does not show an application button.
- **Eligibility evidence:** South African citizenship; Matric Mathematics at least 65%; English HL or FAL at least 65%; tertiary applicants with an overall average above 65%; eligible full-time degrees at selected South African universities. The official page lists fields and capped funding details; see it for the full criteria. Applicants with disabilities, LEAP Science and Maths Schools, and former AWS interns are prioritised.
